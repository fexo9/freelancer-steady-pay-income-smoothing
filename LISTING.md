## The problem

March: $4,200. Three clients paid at once and you felt rich. April: $800 —
and rent is $1,650, due on the 1st. That whiplash isn't bad luck; it's the
normal freelance experience. The money comes in waves, the bills come like
clockwork, and spending what you just earned is what keeps freelancers broke
in good months and panicked in bad ones.

The fix isn't willpower — it's a mechanism. Five independent published sources
(Medium, October 2026; Harpoon) converge on the same one, and every figure
below is theirs, quoted with sources inside the kit — we didn't measure them:

- **@rkdigitalabs:** "Stop spending what you earn. Start paying yourself a
  salary. Every dollar you earn goes into the holding account first... a fixed
  'salary'... based on your average month minus a buffer, never your best
  month."
- **@jaideepsawhney:** "Income smoothing means paying yourself the same amount
  every month from a buffer account, no matter how much you actually earned
  that month." Illustrated: freelance UX writer, income $2,100–$11,800/mo,
  baseline $3,900, 28% to tax, $4,200 salary on the 1st.
- **uxchrisnguyen (13k+ subscribers):** when a payment lands, decide ONCE —
  30% taxes gone immediately, 40% business reserves, 30% personal salary.
  Runway = available cash / monthly burn.
- **@savesmartwithhammad:** per-payday ritual — tax first, then bills,
  savings, down a priority ladder; build a one-month buffer, then pay
  yourself a steady salary from it.
- **Harpoon:** feast months — normal paycheck, not bigger; top up the buffer.
  Famine months — paycheck unchanged, funded by the buffer; chase invoices.

## The kit

That mechanism, implemented as a working spreadsheet system — the formulas do
the math, the checklists enforce the discipline. 11-language Excel workbooks
(EN, ZH, FR, ES, AR right-to-left, RU, DE, JA, PT, HI, IT), 8 sheets each:

- **START** — 4 setup steps, the 7 rules of steady pay, and the cited
  evidence panel with all five sources quoted.
- **INCOME-FLOOR** — enter 3–6 months of income; the sheet computes your
  average, best/worst month, and recommended salary — with floor and
  best-month guard checks so you never size from a peak.
- **BUFFER-ENGINE** — the salary auto-pulls from INCOME-FLOOR; the 12-month
  tracker computes tax moved, net to buffer, salary paid, and the running
  balance with a STRONG / OK / THIN / DANGER status every month.
- **TAX-RESERVE** — per-payment log with formula set-asides and totals, plus
  the quarterly estimated-tax deadline calendar.
- **FEAST-FAMINE-PLAN** — the two 5-step protocols (feast: taxes first,
  refill buffer, no raise; famine: salary unchanged, chase invoices, 5
  prospects a week) with readiness counters.
- **RUNWAY-GAUGE** — available cash ÷ monthly burn, with an automatic
  STRONG / STABLE / THIN / CRITICAL verdict.
- **MONTHLY-REVIEW** — the 30-minute, 8-item monthly checklist with automatic
  completion %.
- **WORKED-EXAMPLE** — Priya's fictional end-to-end run: income swinging
  $2,100–$11,800, salary fixed at $4,200 through a feast and a famine month —
  every number formula-driven, identical in all 11 languages.
